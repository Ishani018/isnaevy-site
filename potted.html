PROJECT · GAME

Potted: a cozy plant nursery.

A pixel-art game about growing flowers, adopting pets and decorating a small home.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Potted is

Potted is a relaxing plant nursery game where you buy seeds, plant them in pots, water them, and watch them bloom through growth stages. You decorate your rooms with paintings and pets, manage an inventory, and earn coins by harvesting flowers. It is built with React Native and Expo, and runs on iOS, Android and web.

Seeds are bought at the nursery with coins and go into your inventory. You drag them onto empty pots to plant them. To water, you drag the watering can over a planted pot and hold for two seconds; watering a seed instantly advances it to the bud stage. Each flower grows through four stages: seed, bud, slight bloom and full bloom. Harvesting at full bloom earns coins to buy more seeds.

03 / WHAT YOU CAN GROW

Rooms, flowers and hanging plants

Three rooms

  • Window shelf: two wooden shelves by the window. Drag pots from the sill, plant seeds and water them.
  • Balcony: hanging pots on curtain rods. Drag pots from the stool and hang your plants.
  • Sunroom: a bright room with shelf pots and hanging hooks.

Seven flowers

Daisy, rose, hydrangea, snapdragon, poppy, marigold and peony.

Five hanging plants

Jasmine, string of pearls, philodendron, petunia and bougainvillea.

04 / THE WORLD AROUND IT

Town, pets and decoration

A town map connects the home, the nursery, a pet adoption shop, a market and a gallery. You can adopt pets, hang paintings on your walls (six are available), and unlock achievements such as “First Bloom” and “Green Thumb”. Room backgrounds change with the seasons, there is looping ambient music with a mute toggle, and progress is saved automatically with AsyncStorage.

05 / UNDER THE HOOD

Tech stack

  • React Native 0.74 and Expo 51 for the cross-platform app and build tooling.
  • React Navigation for screens and transitions.
  • Reanimated 3 and Gesture Handler for the drag-and-drop of pots, seeds and the watering can.
  • AsyncStorage for saved game state, and Expo AV for background music.

The game state lives in a reducer (buy, plant, water, harvest), with separate modules for the growth-tick engine, coordinate projection and the snap points where pots and hooks sit in each room.

Status: in development. The source is on GitHub. Asset files are not included in the repository because of their size. To see why the game is designed the way it is, read the field note “Designing a cozy loop around care, not urgency”.
