Vera: a creative canvas of my own.

A canvas for designing, arranging and bringing ideas to life.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Vera is

Vera is an independent creative canvas for designing, arranging and bringing ideas to life — a more personal take on an all-in-one visual studio. It is the first project from Isnaevy.

02 / HOW I THINK ABOUT IT

The object and its controls

For a design canvas, the work is in the relationship between what you see and the controls that change it. Selection, movement, resizing, text editing, layers and export all shape how directly a person can change a design. I write about this in the field note “Creative tools should make the next action feel possible”.
