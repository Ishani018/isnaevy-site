ABOUT ISNAEVY

A home for things
worth making.

Isnaevy is my independent home for software, games, experiments, and creative tools. It is less a single product and more a growing collection of things I want to make real.

THE IDEA

Curiosity, made tangible.

I’m Ishani, a computer science student and independent maker. I like building at the intersection of technology and visual creativity — turning an idea from a sketch, a strange thought, or a problem I keep noticing into something people can actually use.

Some projects are practical. Some are playful. Some begin as experiments and become much bigger than expected. Isnaevy gives all of them the same home.

The common thread is intentionality: I care about how something works, but also about how it feels to use.

“Small ideas deserve
room to become themselves.”
