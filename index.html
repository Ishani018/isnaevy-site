About

Isnaevy is my home for independent projects — games, interactive experiences, and creative tools, each built solo and given room to become fully itself.

It started with Vera, a creative canvas I'm still shaping, and has grown to include a wish-making experience, a couple of pixel-art games, and a set of small zero-dependency tools for other makers. I write up the decisions and lessons behind them in the field notes.
