PROJECT · MUSIC TOOL

Scoreplay Studio: read it, hear it.

A sheet-music player for practising at your own pace.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Scoreplay Studio is

Scoreplay Studio is a sheet-music player for importing scores, following every part, and hearing the arrangement at your own pace.

02 / WHAT IT DOES

Notation and playback together

  • Import scores from MusicXML or PDF (with PDF recognition).
  • Follow each part on the page.
  • Hear the arrangement through piano playback, slowed down if you need.

The design question is whether notation and playback stay connected: following a part, slowing playback and hearing a passage should support one another. I write about this in the field note “Creative tools should make the next action feel possible”.

Status: in development. Try it at scoreplay-studio.onrender.com.
