Plopper: visual sprite coordinates.

Place sprites visually on your game background and copy exact paste-ready coordinate arrays.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Plopper is

Plopper is a zero-dependency visual sprite placement and coordinate export tool. When building games or web interfaces, positioning sprites by hand coding numbers (x: 230, y: 442) and constantly reloading the game loop is tedious and imprecise. Plopper eliminates trial-and-error by letting you arrange sprites visually on your actual background and instantly copying paste-ready coordinate arrays.

Set your game’s native resolution (e.g. 1376 × 768), drop in your level or room background, and multi-select your sprite assets. You can drag sprites, grab corner handles to resize (aspect-ratio locked), mirror/flip horizontally, adjust z-index stacking order, and nudge by 1px or 10px with keyboard arrows.

03 / TECHNICAL DETAILS

Center-anchored coordinate outputs

Most 2D game engines (like Phaser, Pixi, Godot, or React Native custom layout engines) calculate sprite positions from center anchors. Plopper automatically normalizes and outputs coordinates with center points (x, y), dimensions (w, h), flip boolean, and z-order.

[
  { key: 'flower_pot', x: 230, y: 442, w: 120, h: 110, flip: false, z: 1 },
  { key: 'persian_cat', x: 96,  y: 690, w: 150, h: 140, flip: true,  z: 2 }
]
