PROJECT · GAME · ANDROID

Live a whole life, one month at a time.

Finlit is a free pixel-art life simulator about money. Start at 18 with ₹50,000 and a tiny hostel room. Work, borrow, fall in love, raise a family and retire, and see what every decision really costs.

01 / WORK

Pick your hustle.

Take gigs, climb a career ladder or start your own business. Apply, wait, get rejected, try again. Pay, rank and risk all change what you can afford next month.

  • Gigs, careers and freelance work with real downsides
  • Monthly summaries that explain why your money moved
  • Retire at 58 and live on a pension from your PPF and NPS

02 / WHEELS

Cash, EMI, or keep walking?

Every vehicle in the game, from a bicycle to a private jet. Pay in cash or finance it, then live with the running costs, the resale value and the EMI that never forgets you.

  • 36 vehicles across bikes, cars, boats and air
  • Showroom, second-hand market and your own garage
  • Miss three EMIs and the bank takes the car

03 / HOME

From hostel to dream home.

Start in a hostel room. Rent, pay in cash or take a home loan, move in or stay where you are. Homes you own but don’t live in can earn rent.

  • 24 homes, from shared student rooms to a villa with a home theatre
  • Rent, buy outright or take a 20-year home loan
  • Property tax and upkeep are part of owning

04 / FAMILY

Marriage, babies and a party every year.

Get married, welcome surprise babies and twins, and plan each birthday from a quiet dinner to a full party. Save into the birthday jar so the big day doesn’t hurt.

  • Kids, parents and spouse each have their own needs
  • Adopt a dog, cat or parrot, solo or married
  • Plan solo trips or family holidays across eight destinations

05 / CREDIT

Loans: friend or frenemy?

Your CIBIL score is a living thing. Pay on time and it climbs. Miss an EMI and it drops 30 points. Dispute errors on your report, use the credit builder, and learn what a score unlocks.

  • A full credit report with factors and history
  • Dispute mistakes, with a fee and no guarantee
  • Taxes, loans, cards and the estate you leave behind
