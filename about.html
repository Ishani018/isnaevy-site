ABOUT ISNAEVY

Independent work, made with care.

Isnaevy is the home for the projects I build on my own: games, creative tools, and research experiments. I’m Ishani, and I work on them from India.

01 / THE STUDIO

What Isnaevy is

Isnaevy is an independent studio and portfolio. Each project here is something I designed and built myself, and each one starts from a small question: what would make this easier, calmer, or more fun for the person using it?

The current projects include Vera, a creative canvas for designing and arranging ideas; Potted, a cozy pixel-art plant nursery game; Finlit, a pixel-art financial life simulation; Scoreplay Studio, a sheet-music player; and in-browser utilities like Dropper (video background chroma recoloring) and Plopper (visual sprite coordinate placement). Most are still in development, and the site says so on each project.

02 / HOW I WORK

Small, finished steps

I prefer building something people can try over describing something they might one day use. I write about the decisions behind each project in the journal, including the parts that are uncertain or unfinished.

My other interest is applied research, particularly how we measure and trust systems that use language models. That work shapes how I think about evidence in everything else I make.
