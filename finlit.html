PROJECT · GAME

Finlit: money as part of a life.

A pixel-art life simulation about work, money and everyday choices.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Finlit is

Finlit is a mobile financial-literacy RPG and life simulation in pixel art. The player makes everyday money decisions and sees how they affect a life over time.

02 / SYSTEMS

What the game models

Worksalary & needs
Homehousing & debt
Futureinvesting & retirement

The systems described in the project connect work and salary to everyday needs, health, housing, debt, investing, family events and long-term retirement goals. The aim is to treat money as part of a life rather than a spreadsheet detached from consequences.

03 / DESIGN GOAL

Readable cause and effect

1Player choice
→
2Visible change
→
3Month-end explanation
→
4Better next choice

The hard design problem is making consequences understandable. If the player takes a job, buys groceries, advances a month or buys a home, the game should show what changed and why. I write about this in the field note “Turning money decisions into a life simulation”.

Status: in development. The project README and source are on GitHub.
