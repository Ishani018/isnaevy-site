Dropper: in-browser video recoloring.

Change video background colors right in your browser with real-time WebGL chroma keying.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Dropper is

Dropper is a client-side video background recoloring studio. It lets you change a video’s background color directly in your web browser with zero installs, zero dependencies, no server processing, and no accounts required. Drop a video in, click the old color directly on the preview canvas to eyedrop it, pick a replacement, and export.

Try it online now: Launch the interactive tool in your browser.

02 / FEATURES

Real-time chroma keying & alpha compositing

  • Flat-background MP4s & transparent WebMs: Works directly with character animations, timers, and game loops.
  • Live eyedropper: Click anywhere on the video preview to sample the target chroma color — no manual hex hunting.
  • Real-time WebGL shader: Preview your color changes live as the video plays at full resolution.
  • Tolerance & edge softness sliders: Fine-tune thresholds to ensure crisp silhouettes without color bleed or harsh halos.
  • Dual export modes: Turbo mode (GPU canvas sampling, perfect for 25-minute timers in ~1 minute) and Real-time 30fps capture.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

WebGL shaders & browser APIs

Dropper runs on a custom WebGL fragment shader. For flat-background videos, the shader measures Euclidean distance in RGB color space between each pixel and the sampled background color, applying a smoothstep filter for clean anti-aliased edges. For transparent WebM files, it composites directly over the chosen color using hardware alpha blending.

Exporting leverages the native browser canvas.captureStream() and WebCodecs / MediaRecorder pipelines. Because all operations execute strictly in your local browser sandbox, your video files never leave your computer.
