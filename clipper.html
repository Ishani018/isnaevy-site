Clipper: lossless background remover.

Clean flat backgrounds from sprites and icons in seconds directly in your browser.

01 / OVERVIEW

What Clipper is

Clipper is a zero-dependency in-browser tool for removing flat or solid backgrounds from sprites and icons, exporting crisp transparent PNGs losslessly. Unlike online background removers that re-encode images through lossy cloud servers, Clipper runs 100% locally on your machine via HTML5 Canvas. Only background pixels have their alpha channel set to zero—your artwork is never recompressed.

Launch in your browser: Clean sprites instantly with zero installation.

02 / FEATURES

Precision pixel tools

  • Auto-remove from edges: One-click inward flood fill from all four canvas borders—cleans away backgrounds in a single tap.
  • Magic wand tool: Click any connected background area with customizable flood-fill color tolerance.
  • Color key mode: Erase a sampled hex color across the entire image at once.
  • Eraser & restore brushes: Paint away stubborn artifacts or selectively restore pixels if too much was removed.
  • Edge feathering: Soften boundary transitions to avoid harsh aliasing on dark and light backgrounds.
  • Batch export & zip: Process multiple sprites and download them together in a clean ZIP bundle.

03 / WHY CLIENT-SIDE MATTERS

Lossless fidelity for game assets

In pixel art and 2D sprite rendering, palette consistency and anti-aliasing matter. Cloud background removal tools often apply machine-learning segmentation filters that fuzz sharp edges and crush subtle gradients. Clipper respects your raw raster data: only transparent alpha values are written, preserving original RGB pixel values completely intact.
